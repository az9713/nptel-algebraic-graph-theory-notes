How the arguments connect

Incidence matrices turn connectivity into rank and spanning trees into determinants. Symmetric spectra turn walks and subgraphs into quantitative bounds. Random walks convert the spectral gap into a convergence rate. Cospectral constructions show which structural information the spectrum loses. Hypercubes, expansion and signed graphs then test how far those methods extend.

Independent study edition. Prof. Ranveer Singh’s NPTEL recordings provide the topic spine. Original exposition, source limitations and verification evidence are separately recorded.
